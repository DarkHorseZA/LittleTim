# Privacy Policy for re-Genesis

**Effective date:** 16 June 2026

re-Genesis is a daily companion to the *re-Genesis* book by Theunis Pienaar.
This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with
your information. The short version: the app keeps your practice on your device,
and sends nothing about you anywhere.

## What we collect

**Nothing leaves your device.** re-Genesis has no account system, no login, and
no server that stores your information. Everything you create in the app is
saved only in the app's local storage on your own device, including:

- Your daily practice progress (rituals, MSG and SEE completions)
- Your journal stitches and reflections
- Your patchwork quilt
- Your settings, including the chapter you are reading
- An email address, only if you choose to enter one. It is stored locally on
  your device and is not transmitted to us or anyone else by the current
  version of the app.

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any of this. We cannot see it.

## Analytics and tracking

re-Genesis contains **no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party
tracking** of any kind. There are no usage statistics, no crash reporting
services, and no marketing trackers.

## Network use

The app makes one kind of network request: it fetches publicly available
journal posts from the author's website (`theunispienaar.com`) so you can read
them inside the app. Opening a post loads that public web page in an in-app
viewer.

As with visiting any website, the author's web server may record standard
technical request information such as your IP address and the time of the
request. No information you create in the app, and no personal information, is
attached to or sent with these requests.

## Children's privacy

re-Genesis does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone,
including children.

## Deleting your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you can remove all of it at any
time by deleting the app, or by clearing the app's storage in your device
settings. There is nothing for us to delete on our side, because we never
receive it.

## Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change in a future version (for example, if an
optional newsletter sign-up begins sending your email to a mailing-list
provider), this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the
effective date above will change.
